About

I started out selling door to door.

You learn fast that a no is usually a broken process, not a broken person.

From there I moved into recruitment and HR, then marketing, sales enablement and CRM, and finally RevOps. Different titles, the same job every time: getting sales, marketing, customer success and technology to work as one, and getting people who don't usually sit together into the same room until the work gets done.

The moment it clicked was a quarterly review. Sales blamed marketing. Marketing blamed the data. Customer success heard about churn last. Nobody was failing. The system between them was.

That's the work I care about: sales and marketing married to technology, done well enough that the customer feels it. Not more tools. Fewer gaps.

Along the way I built a commercial function across India, the Middle East and Africa at GlobalData, where I was named Employee of the Year. I worked on pipeline intelligence for IBM DACH, and led CRM transformation at VFS Global across 60+ countries. I'm a certified Salesforce Administrator, Business Analyst and Agentforce Specialist, based in Dubai.

Who I write for

Founders whose business only works when they're in the room. Revenue leaders inheriting a CRM nobody trusts. And the implementation partners who get handed both.
